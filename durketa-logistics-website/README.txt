Files:

- script.js: mobile navigation and contact form behavior

IMPORTANT BEFORE PUBLISHING:
1. Replace the sample/stock truck photos if you have licensed company photos. The current photos load from Unsplash over the internet.
2. In script.js, set businessEmail to your real company email. Until then, the form displays a reminder and does not send inquiries.
3. Review service descriptions and remove anything you do not actually offer.
4. Add your real business phone/email and any required legal/privacy details.
5. Do not add fabricated testimonials, credentials, statistics, or customer logos.
6. This is a static front-end starter. It is not connected to a backend or database.
7. To use a custom domain, the domain must be connected in the hosting platform's domain settings and DNS records updated as instructed by that platform.

To preview:
Open index.html in a modern browser. For best results, upload the full folder to a static hosting service or use your existing website builder's project editor.
